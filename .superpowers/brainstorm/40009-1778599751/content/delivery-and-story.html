Story-driven Growth & Loyalty — How it works

⬆
Boost
Users back apps they believe in. Boosted apps surface more often in delivery results. This is a signal of real user belief — not a vanity like.
+
Follow Creator
Users subscribe to a creator's future app drops. When that creator packages a new app, followers receive it — like a newsletter, not a social feed.
🃏
Story Card as Shareable Unit
Every Story card has a shareable link. Developers can post their card to Twitter, Slack, or anywhere. Users who click land directly in AppDrop's delivery experience for that app.
