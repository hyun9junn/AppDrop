Story Rings — How they work

🔴
Colored ring = unseen
Gradient ring signals a new or unviewed app Story. Drawn from: new drops this week + boosted apps + favorite creators' launches.
⚪
Grey ring = seen
Already viewed Stories fade to grey — exactly like Instagram. Keeps the row fresh and tells users where they left off.
📢
For developers — this is the ad slot
The Story ring row is where developers' apps get promoted. Featured placement in the ring = prime real estate. Boosted apps earn ring placement. Future: paid featured story slots.
